How to play

  1. 1

    Trick Goatir

    Goatir guards a secret passcode. Pick an attack from the hotbar or write your own trick.

  2. 2

    Botir learns

    If the passcode leaks, Botir studies your trick, crafts a new defense, and tests it before switching it on.

  3. 3

    Try again

    Goatir levels up and the passcode changes. Replay your trick and it gets blocked. Find a new way in.

Rehearsal uses simulated agents for a repeatable demo. Live uses real Gemini agents. Starting a new world resets the vault.
